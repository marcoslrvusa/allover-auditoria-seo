How much does a roof cost in Katy?

There is no honest one-price answer. The number depends on six variables — and any roofer who quotes before inspecting is guessing.

FactorWhy it moves the price
Roof sizeMore squares means more material, labor and disposal.
MaterialArchitectural shingle, metal and TPO sit in different price bands.
Pitch and complexitySteep slopes, valleys and dormers slow the crew down.
LayersTwo or more layers require a full tear-off and more dumpsters.
DeckingSoft or rotted decking has to be replaced before the new roof.
Access and cleanupFences, trees and tight lots change the logistics.
What you get before a number: a free inspection, photos of the problem areas, a written scope and a clear explanation of what is optional vs required.

Insurance & storm

Hail and wind claims in Katy

Katy sits in a hail- and wind-prone corridor. When a storm hits, the roof is usually the first thing damaged and the last thing homeowners know how to document.

  • Step 1 — Inspect before you file. We document hail hits, wind creases, granule loss and soft metals.
  • Step 2 — Separate damage from wear. Insurance covers sudden damage, not an aged roof.
  • Step 3 — Build the scope. Photos, measurements and a line-item report for the adjuster.
  • Step 4 — Repair or replace. We match the approved scope and keep you informed.
What we will not do: promise that your claim will be approved. Coverage depends on your policy, deductible and the adjuster’s assessment. What we control is the quality of the documentation and the work.
